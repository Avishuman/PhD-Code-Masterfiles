* The Mathematica files pertain to the continuum elastic simulations of a single Piezo ion channel protein in a plasma membrane.
* Piezo_Arclength_Soln.nb does not consider the effect of any vertical tethering force on the channel activation.
* Piezo_Arclength_Force.nb considers the effect of an axysymmetric vertical force at the Piezo-membrane interface on its gating.
